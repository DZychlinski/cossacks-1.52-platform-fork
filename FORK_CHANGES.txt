Settings, saves and autosaves are kept in the game's own folder.
AI: computer players have lifted or no limits on the buildings they build (details in AI_CHANGES.txt).
Chat messages stay on screen twice as long (20 seconds) and show the game time, e.g. [0:12:34].
The minimap "under attack" marker flashes four times as long.
The resource bar shows how many of your peasants work on each resource, and how many are idle.
New "Normal AI / Insane AI" choice next to the game speed in the single player and multiplayer game setup (host only), see AI_CHANGES.txt.
Mods: put changed data files in mods\<name>\ next to the exe and list the mod under [Mods] Active= in cossacks_native.ini; all players need the same mods.
The game runs fullscreen from the start (borderless, menu included), so the screen no longer switches resolution when a game starts or ends.
A building that is researching still counts as present for requirements, e.g. the 18th century no longer waits for a busy Blacksmith or Academy.
Each new chat message shows the last 10 chat messages again.
Upgrades being researched in your buildings are listed at the top right with percent done.
Unit counts over icons (selected units, formations, units in a mine) show numbers up to 9999 instead of the infinity sign.
A loaded game keeps its capture option (before, everything could be captured after loading). Older saves do not load.
New lobby option "Allow AI to build walls" (additional options, off by default). Lobbies and recordings of older versions are incompatible.
Fixed crashes in long games: units sent to a place outside the map, and units that could not reach their army behind a closed gate.
